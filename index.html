Umvula — Admin Control Centre

Last updated: May 2025
Maintained by: Umvula Development Team
Database: LoginSystem
Applies to:
✅ Admin Accounts ✅ LoginSystem DB ✅ dbo.Users ✅ dbo.Stock ✅ dbo.Transactions

The Umvula Admin System is a browser-based administration platform for managing business users, inventory stock, financial transactions, loan eligibility, and generating financial reports — all backed by the LoginSystem database schema. This documentation provides administrators with a complete walkthrough of every feature, tab, and workflow available in the Control Centre.

Feature Highlights

The following table summarises the key capabilities available to administrators:

Step-by-Step Walkthrough for Administrators

Note: All steps below assume you are logged in with an Admin account. Admin credentials for the demo environment are admin / Admin@123. Business user login has been restricted to the admin interface only.
1
Sign In to the Admin Control Centre
Navigate to the login screen by clicking the "Click here to continue" link on this page. On the login screen, ensure Admin is selected in the role selector (highlighted in teal). Enter your admin username and password. If your credentials are correct and your account is active, you will be redirected to the dashboard automatically.

Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password?" to initiate a 6-digit reset code flow. The code is valid for 15 minutes.
2
Review the Overview Dashboard
The Overview tab is the landing screen after login. It displays six key metric cards at the top: total registered business users, total revenue, total profit, total transactions, loan-eligible users, and pending loan requests.

Below the stats, a line chart aggregates all business users' monthly sales and profit across 12 months (Jul–Jun). At the bottom, individual Business User Cards show each user's latest sales/profit, credit score dial, and loan tier badge. Clicking a card navigates to that user's full profile in the Users tab.
3
Manage Business Users
Navigate to Users in the sidebar. A scrollable list of business users appears on the left panel. Click any user to load their full profile on the right, which includes:

• A 12-month bar chart of their sales and profit
• Their loan qualification scale (Bronze → Platinum tier with maximum loan amount)
• A quarterly income forecast based on recent profit growth trends

Admins can click Edit to modify any user's details, credit score, role, or status. The Email button opens a pre-filled loan offer email. The Report button generates a detailed monthly report for that user.
4
Review Loan Eligibility & Requests
The Loan Eligibility tab lists all users who qualify at Gold or Platinum tier. Qualification is calculated automatically using average monthly profit, credit score, and whether the business shows positive growth.

The Requests tab shows all users who have submitted loan requests. Pending requests display green Approve and red Decline action buttons. The pending request count is shown as a badge on the sidebar navigation item. Resolving a request sends a confirmation toast notification.
5
Administer the Database (dbo.Users)
The Database tab (Admin only) provides full CRUD access to the dbo.Users table. Use the live search field to filter by username, full name, or email. Each row displays the user's role, credit score, loan tier, status (Active/Suspended), and join date.

Click the Edit button to update any field. Click Delete to permanently remove a record (you cannot delete your own account). Use Add Record to insert a new user directly into dbo.Users.
6
Manage Stock (dbo.Stock)
The Stock Table tab displays all inventory records with their full financial breakdown. Columns include: StockId, Supplier, Invoice No, Item Description, Quantity Received, Cost (excl. VAT), Cost (incl. VAT), Margin %, Mark-up %, Selling Price (excl. VAT), and Selling Price (incl. VAT).

Click Add Stock Item to open the stock insertion modal. Enter the supplier, invoice number, item description, quantity, cost (excl. VAT), and target margin percentage. The system automatically calculates VAT-inclusive costs, mark-up percentage, and both selling prices (VAT = 15%). Delete any stock record using the red trash button.
7
Record & Review Transactions (dbo.Transactions)
The Transactions tab shows all point-of-sale records with auto-generated TransactionId and ReceiptNumber. Each record links to a stock item (StockId), records quantity sold, price sold at, sale date, total revenue, and the user who made the sale.

Click Add Transaction to log a new sale. Select the stock item from the dropdown — the selling price is pre-filled automatically from dbo.Stock. Select the sale date and associated user. On saving, the transaction updates the user's monthly revenue and profit figures in dbo.Users.
8
Generate Financial Reports
Reports are accessible from the topbar (Daily, Monthly, Yearly buttons) or from the Reports link in the sidebar. Clicking any report button generates a full summary for all business users, including average sales, average profit, credit scores, loan tiers, and the 10 most recent transactions.

To generate a report for a specific user, navigate to their profile in the Users tab and click the Report button. This generates a month-by-month breakdown of their sales, profit, and margin percentage.

Access & Security

Admin-only access: The login screen is restricted to administrator accounts only. Business user self-service login has been disabled in this version. All user management is handled exclusively through the admin Control Centre.

The following security policies are enforced across the system:

PolicyDetail
Password RequirementsMinimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase letter, 1 number, 1 special character (@$!%*?&)
Role EnforcementAdmin login role selector is required — business user accounts cannot sign in through the admin panel
Session ManagementSessions are stored in sessionStorage and cleared on logout or browser close
Remember MeOptional username persistence stored in localStorage — password is never stored
Password Reset6-digit reset codes expire after 15 minutes — shown in-app for demo purposes
Self-deletionAdministrators cannot delete their own account
Account SuspensionSuspended accounts are blocked from logging in with a clear error message
Demo credentials: Admin username is admin and password is Admin@123. You can also explore pre-seeded user records (thabo, lindiwe, sipho, precious, mpho) from within the admin dashboard.
